---
date: 2026-10-15
summary: Integrations scoped to hosts, clickable terminal links, and mcp-connect in the release.
---

- **Integrations can be limited to chosen Runtime Hosts** — a work
  integration on the work machine only. A narrowed scope cuts off sessions
  already running on other hosts.
- **Links in a session's terminal open in your browser.** A tap shows the
  real target first; Cmd/Ctrl-click opens at once.
- New pixel-drawn Notes and Agents icons on the rail.
- `storm-runtime mcp-connect` ships in the binaries: AI apps connect through
  it with the configuration the key dialog shows. The installer's MCP-only
  option is not offered yet.
