summary: Reach your server away from home through a relay, pair on your network with your password, and firewall rules only with consent.
---

v0.9.0 was tagged but never published; this release carries it.

- **Relays, finished.** The apps reach the server through a relay when its
  own address does not answer, sign in and stay connected over mobile data,
  and use agents, integrations, attachments and Add a device away from home.
  Pairing works through a relay too. A relay can now also run on Cloudflare
  Workers. Large uploads and downloads cross a relay intact.
- **Pairing on your network:** the apps list the Storms they find under
  **On this network**; pick yours and enter the password. Refused on networks
  the computer treats as public.
- **Firewalls:** the installers ask before allowing Storm's port from your
  local network, and only from it; `storm-server status` reports the firewall.
- **`storm-runtime mcp-connect`:** one configuration for AI apps that works
  at home and away, and checks the server's identity before sending the key.
- Disconnecting a device in the app now signs it out on the server.
