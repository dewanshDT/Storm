---
date: 2026-11-12
summary: A redesigned app — a window-wide bar with back and forward, an Agents sidebar that sorts by what needs you, one strip for every decision, an inspector with an Agents tab, a simpler launcher, Trash with time left, and Agents & hosts as one grid.
---

- **A bar across the top of the window.** On a desktop and on the web a
  44px bar now runs above everything, with **back and forward** (⌘[ ⌘]
  on a Mac, Alt+← → elsewhere) and the health dot, which moves there from
  the rail.
  - On a Mac the traffic lights sit in the bar. On Windows and Linux the
    window has no system title bar and Storm draws minimise, maximise and
    close. On Linux they follow your desktop's button order.
  - Drag the bar's empty space to move the window, and double-click it to
    maximise or zoom.
  - A phone has no bar.
  - Pages, Settings and the Agents overview now use the window's full width.
- **The Agents sidebar shows what needs you.**
  - One header row: **Agents** opens the overview, and **＋** starts a
    session (⇧⌘N while you're in Agents; in Notes it still makes a folder).
  - Only a session that needs you (a permission prompt, a question or a
    delete to approve) gets an outline. It's the only thing the rail and
    the phone's place bubble count.
  - The line under each name says why in words, such as *permission
    prompt · 3m*, *blocked · …*, *host offline · last seen 9m* or *2
    commits to apply*, instead of tags.
  - Sessions sort by urgency (needs you, blocked, failed, host offline, out
    of usage), and the order holds still while your pointer is in the
    sidebar. Trees and crews move as one, and ENDED starts folded.
  - A dot marks output you haven't seen on this device.
  - A row's **···** menu has **Add to crew…**, and a crew's menu has **Add
    sessions…**.
- **One strip for every decision.** A strip under the session's header now
  asks for everything that needs you, one at a time ("NEEDS YOU · 1 OF 2").
  It replaces the delete dialog that opened on every device, the NEEDS YOU
  card, and the End tree and Dismiss tree banners.
  - An agent's delete request shows who asked, what the agent says word for
    word, and what goes. **Move to Trash** approves it, and **Later** (or
    Esc) leaves it waiting.
  - The approval says how long that vault's Trash keeps it, rather than a
    fixed 30 days.
  - Other screens and devices get one notice, *"… wants to delete … ·
    Review"*, instead of a dialog.
  - On a phone the strip is compact, and **Review** opens the request as a
    sheet.
- **The session inspector has an icon rail:** Context, Wrote, **Agents**,
  About, and Work for a child with its own copy.
  - Closing the panel keeps the rail and its counts visible.
  - The new **Agents** tab lists the agents a session started, with their
    state, reason and work to apply. It also shows exactly what it sent
    them, with each round's stats.
  - **About** keeps Crew, Role, Starts agents, Ran as and Started by, plus
    one line saying what the session can do (*Can: writes to personal · 2
    integrations*).
  - **Starts agents** is a chip in the header.
  - The terminal says how to move focus out of it.
- **A simpler launcher.** It has three fields: **From**, **Agent**, and
  **Where** (workspace and host together, with **New workspace…** inline).
  - Chips under them show what the session can do. Tap one to change it.
  - **More** holds the model, permission mode, crew and role, and the full
    network and integrations sentence.
  - Rows no longer appear and disappear when you change the agent. One that
    doesn't apply is disabled and says why.
- **Trash.**
  - Each item shows how long is left before it's deleted for good, who
    deleted it, and the agent's reason.
  - Select several to restore them or delete them forever. Restore doesn't
    ask, and Delete forever and Empty trash confirm in the strip.
  - How long each vault keeps deleted items is now set in **Settings ›
    Vaults** (*Trash keeps*: 7, 30 or 90 days, or never).
- **Settings › Agents & hosts.** Hosts & default agent and Agents are now
  one page: a grid of your hosts against each agent.
  - Each cell says one thing, such as Ready, Sign in, Update, Install, or
    Can't install with the reason, and clicking it shows the fix.
    **Sign in in a Shell** opens a Shell on that host with the agent's
    login command already typed; you press Enter.
  - Click an agent's name to make it the default.
  - The selected host has its limits as steppers, plus **Rename**,
    **Revoke…** and **Remove…**.
  - A revoked host can be **re-enrolled** under the same name and limits,
    or removed from the list. Its ended sessions keep its name.
  - Hosts now report whether each agent is signed in. Storm runs the
    agent's own status command, or checks that its login file exists, and
    never reads credentials.
- **Smaller changes:**
  - The Settings list no longer has Beta tags, and **Integrations** shows
    your plugins' suggestions as rows, with **Use your own OAuth app…** as a
    button.
  - **AI access** now says what's true: a deleted note goes to its vault's
    Trash, and an agent's delete always waits for you.
  - SlowFlow earth's accent is a step deeper, so selected chips, tabs and
    buttons are easier to read.
- **Known gaps:**
  - The ⌘K search field in the bar is hidden for now. ⌘K still searches
    the current vault.
  - A host can't tell a session whose agent is blocked waiting on another
    session apart from any other blocked one, and usage limits don't say
    when they reset yet. The row says *blocked* or *out of usage* without
    the detail.

Upgrade storm-server, storm-runtime on every host, and the apps. storm-relay
is unchanged. A host still on v0.15.0 keeps working, but **Sign in in a
Shell** isn't offered for it and it reports no sign-in state. Update it from
**Agents & hosts**. The server adds to `agent.db` on its own at first start,
with no step for you: two tables (`session_child_work`, `removed_hosts`) and
a column (`host_enrollments.host_id`). These changes only add, and
`auth.db`, `gateway.db` and each vault's `index.db` are unchanged. Revoking
a host now keeps its limits, so a re-enrolled host gets them back. Removing
a host deletes them.
