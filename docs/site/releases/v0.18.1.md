date: 2026-11-20
summary: A hidden terminal catches up its light/dark theme when shown again, and a sign-in in progress no longer stalls other sessions' views.
channel: stable
---

- **A hidden terminal catches up its theme.** A session's own light/dark
  answer only ever updated through the one view that stayed mounted
  through an app theme switch; a session you weren't looking at kept the
  old brightness until its program happened to ask again on its own.
  Showing that session now catches it up at once.
- **A sign-in in progress no longer stalls other sessions.** Checking a
  password held a lock that every session view also needed, so a slow
  sign-in could leave the whole session list waiting behind it under
  load. Session views no longer wait on that lock.

Upgrade storm-server, storm-runtime on every host, and the apps. No schema
change: `auth.db`, `gateway.db` and each vault's `index.db` are unchanged.
