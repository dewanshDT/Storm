Storm keeps your notes as plain Markdown on a server you run, syncs them to
the macOS, Android and web apps (offline included), runs coding agents on your
own machines, and gives AI apps access to your notes through MCP.

## Start here

- **[Quickstart](/docs/start/quickstart/)** — from nothing to a server, your
  first device and your phone.
- **[Concepts](/docs/start/concepts/)** — the words the rest of these docs use:
  vaults, devices, Runtime Hosts, sessions, integrations.
