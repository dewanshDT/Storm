R2Redesign round 2 · fixing the places that look bolted on
Each frame below replaces a surface in Storm As Built, and its caption names the pattern it fixes. Five things hold across all of them:
  • One decision strip. Anything that needs you to decide appears as one strip under the header, never a modal, and never more than one at a time.
  • One inspector tab per subject.
  • Settings where you'd look for them.
  • No internals by default.
  • Every gesture has a visible equivalent.
  • One sidebar header. The Agents sidebar starts with a single row: "Agents" opens the overview, and a compact ＋ starts a new session (⇧⌘N). It replaces the title, the full-width New session button and the Overview row.
  • One app bar. Every desktop frame has the full-width 44px bar from Window Chrome B: window buttons, back and forward, a global ⌘K field and the health dot. The OS title bar and the sidebar's own search field are gone.
The Agents sidebar follows Agents Sidebar Rules variant A: an outline for needs you, the reason in the sub-line, and a count on the rail. Every hard rule in the brief stands. Deletes always ask. "Runs as" shows on every host. Spawning acts at once and is logged. Crews never grant access. Tokens only.

Open questions
  1. The delete request on other devices. The strip appears only in the session it belongs to, and every device gets the rail count. Today a modal opens on every device. Dropping it means a person who isn't looking at Storm learns of a request only from the count, until push notifications exist.
  2. Sign in from Agents & hosts. "Sign in…" would open a Shell session on that host with the login command already typed. The spec had "Open a shell on this host"; it was never built.
  3. The decision queue. "1 of 2" assumes the server can list pending decisions for a session in order.
  4. Re-enroll and Remove for hosts. Both need server work. Revoke stays as it is.
  5. Round stats ("Round 2 · 14s · 12.3k in") are gone from the default views. Should they live in About under a "Show usage" toggle, or only in Activity?
