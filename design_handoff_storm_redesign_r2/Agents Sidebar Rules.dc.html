Agents sidebar · one rule per signal
A is the recommendation. A red outline means an agent is waiting for you, and only that raises the count on the rail and the bubble. The badge on the mark shows what the agent is doing: a pulse for working, a ring for idle, danger for failed. The sub-line says why it stopped, in words. B is the same, except that failed is outlined too. Rows sort as needs you → blocked → failed (for one hour) → out of usage → working → idle → parked. A row only moves when it enters or leaves the top four, and the order freezes while the pointer is in the sidebar. Children stay under their parent, and the tree moves as one. The sub-line drops to "workspace · age", and children lose "↳ from".

The rule set
Row outline: waiting for you
A 1px solid danger outline on a faint danger tint. Used for a permission prompt, a question, or a delete approval. It's the only thing that raises the count on the rail and the bubble. Solid, because dashed already means lineage.
Mark badge: what it's doing
Working: a filled accent badge that pulses. Idle: an accent ring. Starting: a filled text3 badge. Failed: a filled danger badge. Parked and out of usage: no badge, with the name in text2.
Sub-line: why, in words
"permission prompt · 3m", "blocked · waiting on test-gateway", "failed · host restarted", "out of usage · resets 15:00". Otherwise "workspace · age". The host shows only when it isn't one the crew header already names. There are no tag pills and no amber.
Roll-up when collapsed
A collapsed crew or tree takes on its most urgent state. "1 needs you" adds the outline. "1 failed" turns the count text danger in A; in B it adds the outline too. ENDED stays collapsed, showing its count and Dismiss all.
Sorting
Crews sort among crews, and loose trees within RUNNING, by their most urgent member. Within a crew, the attention tier sorts first and your drag order holds below it. Within a tree, children sort the same way under their parent.
New in these frames · not built today
• Collapsing a tree (the parent's chevron). Today a tree is always open.
• The idle ring. Today the build shows no badge when idle, and the chip reads Running.
• Server-side attention reasons ("blocked · waiting on …", "resets 15:00"). The spawn models already carry blocked and usage_limit; "waiting on" and the reset time may not exist yet.
• The rail and bubble count.
