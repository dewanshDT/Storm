The window's top · round 2
The last attempt still looked bolted on, because the window buttons were dropped into a cell of Storm's grid. Three columns still met at the top, each with its own background and its own line, and the buttons had to fit around them. Mac apps that feel native do the opposite: the buttons belong to a surface, and the grid starts after that surface. A gives them to the sidebar. The rail and the sidebar become one left panel, full height, one colour, with one vertical line in the whole window. The buttons sit in its top-left corner, and the main pane's top row is a quiet toolbar with no line under it until content scrolls under it. B gives them to a full-width app bar that carries something real: back, forward and a global ⌘K. Only the top of each window is drawn.

Spec for B (chosen)
The bar
44px tall, full width, on bg (the rail's colour, so bar and rail read as one L-shaped frame, as in Slack, VS Code and Teams), with a 1px border line under it. It's the only full-width line in the window. On macOS the window buttons sit at x 20, centred. Back and forward follow at 18px, and the health dot sits at the right end; it's the same dot that's on the rail today, which moves here. Blank space in the bar drags the window, and double-clicking it zooms.
⌘K field
Centred, 420px wide, shrinking to 280 at narrow widths. It uses bg with a border outline and rControl radius. The placeholder reads "Search notes, jump to a session…", with ⌘K on the right. Clicking it or pressing ⌘K opens the palette in place. The palette isn't designed yet, and it needs search across vaults (new server work).
Below the bar
Rail, sidebar and main pane as today. The sidebar's own search field goes away, since ⌘K replaces it. "Agents" or the vault name heads the list, with ＋. The main header keeps its line.
Windows and Linux
The same bar. Caption buttons sit at its right end, three 46px buttons the full height of the bar, with close red on hover, and the health dot moves left of them. Linux follows the desktop's button order. On web, the browser owns the window, so the bar starts with back and forward.
States
Not focused: the buttons go grey and the field turns text3. Full screen: the buttons go away and back and forward move to x 20. The bar never hides, because the ⌘K field is the way in.
Phone
No bar. Search stays in the bubbles and the pill, as v2 has it.
Spec for A (not chosen, kept for reference)
One left panel
The rail and the sidebar share surface and run the full height. The line between them is gone; spacing and the selected icon's accentSoft pill carry the split. There's one vertical line in the window, between the panel and the main pane. The panel's top 52px holds the window buttons at x 20, centred, with the collapse toggle at the panel's right end.
The main toolbar
52px, on bg, with no line under it while the content is at the top. A 1px line fades in once the content scrolls under it, as in Finder and Notes. The title is 15/600 on the same centre line as the buttons. The status chip loses its pill and becomes a dot and a word, which is quieter in a toolbar.
The list header
"Agents" moves out of the top row and becomes the first line of the list, with ＋ beside it in place of the full-width New session button. So the window buttons own the corner and the list starts below them.
Behaviour
Blank space in the panel's top row and the toolbar drags the window, and double-clicking it zooms. When the window isn't focused, the buttons go grey and the title turns text2. In full screen the buttons go away and the panel's top row shrinks to 12px. When the sidebar collapses, the buttons and the toggle move into the toolbar.
Windows and Linux
The same panel and toolbar. The panel's top row holds only the toggle. Caption buttons sit at the toolbar's right end: three 46px buttons, the full height of the row, with close red on hover. Linux follows the desktop's button order.
Optional, macOS only
The left panel could use the system sidebar material (vibrancy), so the desktop shows faintly through it, as in Finder. It's off by default: tokens stay the source of colour, and the three themes must look the same everywhere.
